Biometric Data & AI Tracking Notice

This application utilizes computer vision (MTCNN face detection and FaceNet deep embeddings) to track passengers across sequential interior bus photos.

Mandatory Compliance Notice:
  • Face embeddings are sensitive biometric representations used solely for journey occupancy and attendance tracking.
  • Raw biometric vectors are never exported in public CSV reports.
  • First-seen and last-seen timestamps are observation estimates, not guaranteed exit/boarding times.
